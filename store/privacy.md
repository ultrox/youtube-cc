# Privacy: My Subtitles for YouTube

This extension has no server and no analytics. It sends nothing to its author or to third parties,
and it makes no network requests of its own.

- **What it reads:** on youtube.com, the video player's lists of subtitle and audio tracks and which
  ones are playing.
- **What it changes:** the player's subtitle track, when you pick a language; and, while "Original
  audio" is on, a dubbed audio track to the video's original one.
- **What it stores, on your device only:** the list of languages you chose (language codes such as
  "de" or "en") and whether "Original audio" is on, in the browser's extension storage.

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

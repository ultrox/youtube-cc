# Privacy: My Subtitles for YouTube

This extension has no server and no analytics. It sends nothing to its author or to third parties.
Its only network request goes to YouTube itself: with "Dual subtitles" on, it asks youtube.com for the
second language's subtitles of the video you are watching, the same request YouTube's player makes for
the first.

- **What it reads:** on youtube.com, the video player's lists of subtitle and audio tracks and which
  ones are playing.
- **What it changes:** the player's subtitle track, when you pick a language; while "Original
  audio" is on, a dubbed audio track to the video's original one; and, while "Dual subtitles" is on,
  how the subtitles are drawn (two lines instead of YouTube's one).
- **What it stores, on your device only:** the list of languages you chose (language codes such as
  "de" or "en"), whether "Original audio" and "Dual subtitles" are on, and the two languages you
  paired, in the browser's extension storage.

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
